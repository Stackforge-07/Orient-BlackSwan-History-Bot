Darshi Thoradeniya, Senior Lecturer, Department of History,

How have individual women’s bodies fared within the twentieth-century 
Sri Lankan stories of development? In A Critical History of Women’s 
Health in Modern Sri Lanka, Darshi Thoradeniya traces women’s health 
in Sri Lanka from the initial days of birth control and family planning, 
to development and population control, to the militarisation and 
financialisation of women’s bodies. Questioning this ‘success story’, 
she shows, through meticulous research, interviews, policies and 
advertisements, and oral narratives, how women’s bodies were used 
for the nation-building project of post-independence Sri Lanka while 
individual women’s unique bodily and medical experiences were silenced.
